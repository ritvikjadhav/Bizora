Bizora helps local businesses build an online presence, display products and services, and learn about digital tools. This document explains the general rules for using Bizora and the way information may be handled. Please review it carefully before creating an account or publishing a business profile.

01

Privacy Policy

How information is collected, used, and managed.

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled through Bizora's website and related features. The information processed depends on how you use the platform and which details you choose to provide.

Who this policy applies to

It applies to people who create accounts, business owners who manage profiles, and visitors who browse publicly available business information.

Public business information

If a business owner publishes a profile, information intended for public display may be visible to visitors. This can include the business name, description, category, contact details, address, logo, links, and published products or services, depending on the fields completed and platform settings.

Before publishing: Only provide contact details and business information that you are comfortable making public. Do not add private customer information to a public profile.

02

Information We Handle

The types of information that may be processed.

Account information

When you register or sign in, Bizora and its authentication provider may process information such as your email address, account identifier, and name if you provide one. Authentication data is used to manage access to your account.

Business and catalogue information

Business owners may submit details such as business name, category, contact name, phone number, WhatsApp number, address, description, logo, website or social links, and product or service details.

Technical information

The website or its service providers may process limited technical information needed to deliver the service, maintain reliability, protect accounts, and diagnose errors. The exact information depends on the hosting, database, authentication, and browser services in use.

Information you should not submit

Do not submit passwords belonging to other services, payment card details, bank credentials, UPI PINs, one-time passwords, or sensitive personal information that Bizora does not specifically need.

03

How Information Is Used

Why information is processed and when it may be shared.

Service operation

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, save business profiles, manage products and services, display published pages, provide requested features, and maintain platform functionality.

Safety and reliability

Information may also be used to identify technical problems, prevent misuse, protect the platform, respond to support requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Service providers

Bizora may rely on third-party services for functions such as authentication, database storage, hosting, or file storage. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

Public visibility and legal requirements

Published business details may be visible to anyone who can access the public profile. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to address security incidents, protect rights, or respond to lawful requests.

Retention

Information may be retained while an account or business profile is active and for a reasonable period afterward when needed for security, resolving disputes, complying with legal obligations, or maintaining legitimate service records. Actual retention depends on the applicable system and legal requirements.

Bizora should not sell personal information as a product. If the platform introduces new data uses or third-party integrations, this policy should be updated before those changes are relied upon.

Security & Your Choices

Managing your information and protecting your account.

Reasonable safeguards

Bizora is intended to use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect information. However, no website, internet connection, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your account and published profile

  • Use a strong, unique password and keep it confidential.
  • Sign out when using a shared or public device.
  • Review your public profile before publishing it.
  • Keep your contact details and business information accurate.
  • Contact the platform operator if you suspect unauthorised access.

Access, correction, and deletion requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. Some information may need to be removed through account or business-management features, while other requests may require assistance from the platform operator.

Children's privacy

Bizora is intended for people who can legally use the service and manage a business account under applicable law. The platform is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use it.

Policy updates

This policy may change as Bizora develops or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use after a change takes effect may be subject to the revised policy, as permitted by law.
