UPI & Payment Safety

Learn how to recognize common digital payment scams and protect your business payments.

Fake Payment Screenshots

Never rely only on a screenshot or SMS. Check your own payment app or bank statement to confirm that money arrived.

Unexpected Collect Requests

Read payment requests carefully. You generally do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

QR Code Scams

Scanning a QR code and entering your UPI PIN can authorize a payment. Check the transaction details before approving.

Trust & Reputation

Build customer trust with polite review requests, helpful responses, and reliable service.

Build lasting trust

  • Be clear about pricing, delivery, and return policies.
  • Ask all customers honestly for feedback.
