A FEW RULES WORTH REMEMBERING

Stay safe as you grow digitally.

01

Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.

Treat authentication details as private. You don't need to share your PIN to receive money.

02

Verify payments independently.

Check your official payment app or bank account rather than relying only on screenshots or announcements.

03

Protect customer information.

Collect only the information you need and don't publish private customer details without permission.

04

Check before paying for a service.

Review provider identity, eligibility, pricing, renewal terms and cancellation conditions before committing.
