UPI & QR payments Essential safety

Protect your business from payment scams

Digital payments are convenient, but vendors should know how to spot fake payment confirmations, suspicious requests and common QR-code tricks.

  • Check your bank or UPI app before handing over goods.
  • A payment screenshot or SMS alone is not proof of payment.
  • You do not need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.
  • Never share your PIN, OTP or banking credentials.
  • If money is lost to a suspected cyber fraud in India, report it promptly through official channels.
